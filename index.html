📏 Tomorrow's Room Measurement & Photo Checklist

1. Quick Tape-Measure Checklist (nearest 1/4")

  • Wall Width & Height: Left corner to right corner, and floor to ceiling.
  • Trim Heights: Baseboard height (usually 3.5"–5.5") and crown molding (if any).
  • Bed / Headboard: Distance from left wall, total width, and height from floor.
  • Windows & Doors: Distance from left wall, total width (including outer trim), sill height, and top height.
  • Switches / Outlets / Shelves: Any wall plates you want the stripes to clear cleanly.

2. How to Take a True-Scale Wall Photo

  • Stand centered across the room: Hold your phone level at chest height (~4 ft off the floor) facing straight at the wall.
  • Use the standard 1x lens: Avoid 0.5x ultra-wide mode, which bows straight walls near the edges.
  • The 48" Painter's Tape Trick (Pro Tip): Before taking the photo, stick two small pieces of painter's tape (or sticky notes) horizontally on the wall exactly 48 inches (4 ft) apart. When you upload the photo in Photo Overlay mode, click Calibrate Scale and tap both pieces of tape — every stripe & stitch immediately locks to exact real-world size!

3. Vertical Stripe Stencil Tips

  • Check the Min Bridge readout: for delicate wallpaper-scale stitches, a 7–10 mil mylar stencil cut on a Cricut/Silhouette handles bridges down to ~0.12"; if hand-cutting with an X-Acto knife, aim for 0.20"–0.25"+.
  • Use a laser level (or plumb line) along the ceiling to mark stripe centers every Stripe Spacing inches, and align the dashed blue ghost stitch at the top of your stencil over the last painted stitch as you move down each column.
